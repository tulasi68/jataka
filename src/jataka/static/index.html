Let's get the details right.

One question at a time. Each answer is checked, and you can go back to correct anything. After confirmation, Jataka requests planetary positions and presents them in a South Indian Rāśi chart with basic traditional explanations. The initial ayanāṃśa standard is Lahiri (Chitrapaksha); astronomical values remain explicitly unvalidated.

Privacy: answers are kept in this browser session until you confirm. When you confirm, the birth date, local time, selected coordinates and time zone are sent to the calculation service for this request; they are not saved to an account by this app. Refreshing clears the form.
